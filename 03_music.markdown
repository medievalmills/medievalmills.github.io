Outside my academic work, I'm also a brass musician, and play trumpet, cornet, and flugelhorn, as well as dabbling on the much less well-known E♭ [soprano cornet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soprano_cornet). I do a lot of work with local brass bands, and have in the past conducted [Honiton Band](https://honitonband.co.uk), as well as playing with [Exeter Railway](https://www.facebook.com/ExeterRailwayBand/), [Durnovaria Silver](https://durnovariasilver.band), and [Lyme Regis](https://www.lymeregistownband.co.uk). I've also conducted the on-the-day scratch band at [Bandamonium](https://www.bandamonium.co.uk/), an annual festival of brass band music in West Devon.

Over the years, I've also played in a variety of pit bands and ensembles, most recently Sidmouth Amateur Dramatics Society's panto band, and before that Exeter University's Gilbert and Sullivan Society and Footlights.
